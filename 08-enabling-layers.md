**Green materials & low-carbon procurement marketplaces** · `🟠 demonstration`

Databases and marketplaces that put EPD-backed carbon data next to price at the moment of specification and purchase, letting buyers choose low-carbon steel, cement, and other materials and converting Buy Clean-style policies into actual demand signals.

| Company | Type | What they do | Signal / status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Building Transparency (EC3)](https://www.buildingtransparency.org/tools/ec3/) | incumbent | Hosts EC3, the only free, open-access global embodied-carbon calculator with a huge digitized EPD database used in material specification and procurement. | Nonprofit formed 2020; observes 30%+ carbon reductions where EC3 is used to procure; backbone of US Buy Clean implementation |
| [2050 Materials](https://2050-materials.com/) | startup | London-based API/platform aggregating sustainability data on 150,000+ construction products so architects and contractors can compare and specify low-carbon options. | Accelerator-backed early-stage; largest open construction-materials sustainability database claim |
| [Vanilla Steel](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/vanilla_steel) | startup | Berlin B2B digital marketplace auctioning European steel (surplus/excess prime), a digital channel relevant to sourcing lower-carbon and circular steel. | $4.1M raised (seed led by June Fund); building AI price/demand forecasting with EU co-funding |
| [Climate Earth](https://climateearth.com/partner/building-transparency-ec3-tool/) | startup | Automated EPD generation for ready-mix concrete producers, feeding verified low-carbon concrete data into EC3 and procurement decisions. | EC3 ecosystem partner serving concrete producers across North America |
| [Skanska](https://foresight.skanska.com/decarbonizing-construction/article-EC3/) | incumbent | Global contractor that co-created EC3 (with Microsoft) and applies embodied-carbon-based procurement on major projects. | EC3 originated from Skanska's 2019 initiative; now industry-standard practice at scale |

> **White space:** Few transactional players because green premiums are uncertain, materials trade is regional and commoditized, and the best data infrastructure (EC3) is free and nonprofit — hard to monetize a standalone marketplace. The opening is the transaction layer: book-and-claim certificate systems and offtake aggregation for green steel/cement tied to Buy Clean and CBAM price signals, embedded into existing procurement/ERP and estimating software rather than new destinations.

**CBAM & carbon border compliance software** · `🟡 scaling`

Software that collects installation-level embedded-emissions data from non-EU producers and automates EU CBAM reporting and certificate cost forecasting — turning the world's first carbon border tariff (definitive phase from 2026) into a procurement data problem.

|---|---|---|---|
| [CarbonChain](https://www.carbonchain.com/blog/introducing-carbonchain-cbam-supplier-catalogue) | startup | Commodity-grade emissions accounting (metals, energy, agri) with a CBAM Supplier Catalogue — shared infrastructure for installation-level emissions data exchange between operators and EU importers. | Supports ~35% of steel, ~40% of aluminium and ~50% of fertiliser EU import volumes; 1,750+ declarations covering 7.5 Mt of goods over three years |

> **White space:** The Oct 2025 Omnibus simplification (50-tonne de minimis) exempted ~182,000 mostly-SME importers while keeping 99% of emissions in scope — gutting the long-tail SME software market but deepening enterprise and supplier-side need. Openings: tooling for non-EU producers (where default values now cost real money), integration into customs/ERP rails rather than standalone portals, and expansion to UK CBAM (2027) and other emerging border regimes.
