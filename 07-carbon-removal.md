## Carbon Removal & Capture

Pulling CO2 back out of the air and smokestacks and locking it away — or turning it into products. The clearest innovation gap in climate: the IEA pathway needs ~1,000 Mt/yr of removal by 2050 and today's installed base captures a rounding error of that, at $100–1,000/tonne. Almost the entire sector currently lives off voluntary buyers and tax credits.

### Direct Air Capture

**Liquid solvent DAC (hydroxide capture + calciner regeneration)** · `🟠 demonstration`

Giant fan-driven contactors absorb CO2 into a potassium hydroxide solution that is regenerated through a 900C calcium-looping calciner, favoring very large centralized plants that can leverage oil-and-gas engineering and economies of scale.

**River and watershed liming (river alkalinity enhancement)** · `🔵 pilot`

Crushed limestone is dosed into acidified rivers via automated dosers, simultaneously repairing acid-rain damage and boosting the river's natural ability to pull CO2 from the air before the alkalinity reaches the sea.

| Company | Type | What they do | Signal / status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [CarbonRun](https://frontierclimate.com/writing/carbonrun) | startup | Canadian spinout (Dalhousie) running automated limestone dosers on rivers, pioneering 'river alkalinity enhancement' as a distinct CDR pathway with a credible sub-$100/t trajectory. | $25.4M Frontier offtake (Stripe, Shopify, McKinsey, Meta, Google) to remove 55,442 t 2025-2029; generated world's first river alkalinity enhancement credits; +$1M Stripe R&D grant. |

An opening exists for credible MRV-first entrants in geographies with many acidified or neutral-pH rivers (Scandinavia, Appalachia, Canada).

**Coastal enhanced weathering (olivine sand placement)** · `🔵 pilot`

Olivine sand is placed in the nearshore where wave energy grinds and dissolves it, generating bicarbonate alkalinity that captures CO2 while doubling as shoreline-erosion protection — marine enhanced rock weathering powered by free wave action.

| Company | Type | What they do | Signal / status |

> **White space:** Thin field with one clear lead. **Novel storage media (in-situ mineralization, CO2 hydrates, enhanced coal-bed storage, engineered caverns)** · `🔵 pilot`

| [Graphenstone](https://www.graphenstone.com/) | scaleup | Spanish manufacturer of graphene-reinforced lime paints and coatings that reabsorb CO2 as the lime carbonates during curing. | Sold in dozens of countries; one of the most widely distributed 'CO2-absorbing paint' brands with Cradle-to-Cradle certification. |

> **White space:** Sparse field because in-service CO2 uptake is diffusion-limited, slow, and hard to measure — MRV and prescriptive concrete codes block credit monetization; the opening is an additive with third-party-verified uptake tied to an approved carbon-credit methodology, sold through existing admixture giants (Sika, Master Builders, Mapei) rather than direct.

**Biogenic CO2 mineralization into building products (biocement/bio-concrete)** · `🔵 pilot`

Microalgae or bacteria biologically precipitate calcium carbonate that binds aggregate into blocks, tiles and panels, locking photosynthetically captured CO2 into masonry without Portland cement — an extension variation added for completeness.

| Company | Type | What they do | Signal / status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Prometheus Materials](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/prometheus-materials) | startup | CU Boulder spinout using photosynthetic microalgae to grow a zero-carbon biocement for masonry units, pavers and acoustic panels (ProZERO). | Backed by Microsoft's Climate Innovation Fund and Autodesk Foundation; SOM design partnership; bio-concrete supplied to the New York Climate Exchange campus project (2025). |
| [Biomason](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/biomason) | scaleup | Grows Biolith tiles and precast units with carbonate-precipitating bacteria at ambient temperature, replacing fired and cement-bound products. | ~$36.7M Series D (Jan 2025) on top of a $65M Series C; new CEO from the cement industry after restructuring and repeated layoffs — scaling has been rocky. |
| [Minus Materials](https://www.minusmaterials.com/) | startup | CU Boulder spinout cultivating coccolithophore microalgae to grow biogenic limestone intended as a carbon-neutral feedstock for portland cement. | Pre-seed/lab stage; limited public activity since 2023. · *status unverified* |

> **White space:** Biology is slow and nutrient/bioreactor costs are high against $100/t concrete, so few players survive scale-up (see Biomason's turbulence); the near-term opening is high-margin architectural, acoustic and facade products licensed into existing precast plants rather than owned commodity production.
