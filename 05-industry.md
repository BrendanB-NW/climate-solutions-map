### Green Iron, Steel & Metals

**Hydrogen direct reduced iron (H2-DRI) plants and green iron/HBI developers** · `🟠 demonstration`

**Molten oxide electrolysis (MOE) of iron ore** · `🔵 pilot`

Electricity is passed through iron ore dissolved in a ~1,600°C molten oxide bath using an inert anode, splitting ore directly into liquid iron and oxygen with zero process CO2 when run on clean power.

| Company | Type | What they do | Signal / status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Boston Metal](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/03/12/3041404/0/en/Boston-Metal-Commissions-Industrial-Scale-Cell-in-Crucial-Green-Steel-Milestone.html) | scaleup | MIT spinout running inert-anode MOE cells for green steel in Woburn, MA, plus a Brazilian subsidiary applying MOE to recover high-value metals (Nb, Ta, Sn) from mining waste as an early revenue wedge. | ~$370M raised including $262M Series C (ArcelorMittal, Microsoft, BHP among investors); industrial-scale green-steel cell commissioned March 2025; Brazil 10 ktpa high-value-metals plant slated for 2026; first steel demonstration plant planned 2026 |

> **White space:** Effectively a one-company field: inert anodes must survive 1,600°C corrosive oxide melts and the process needs very cheap firm clean power, so nobody else has followed Boston Metal out of the lab. Openings are alternative anode materials/chemistries and Boston Metal's own playbook — monetizing high-margin ferroalloys and critical metals first, where $/tonne tolerance is far higher than commodity steel.

**Low-temperature aqueous/electrochemical ironmaking (iron electrowinning)** · `🔵 pilot`

Iron ore is dissolved in acid or alkaline solution and pure iron is electroplated out at 60-110°C, letting intermittent renewables make iron without coke ovens, DR-grade pellets, or hydrogen infrastructure.

|---|---|---|---|

| [ArcelorMittal + John Cockerill (Volteron)](https://johncockerill.com/en/press-and-news/news/arcelormittal-john-cockerill-announce-volteron/) | incumbent | Industrializing the EU Siderwin alkaline iron-electrowinning process into Volteron, the first industrial-scale low-temperature iron electrolysis plant. | Siderwin pilot completed under Horizon 2020; first 40,000-80,000 tpa plant targeted to start production in 2027, with 0.3-1 Mtpa expansion intended |
| [Element Zero](https://www.mining.com/fortescue-ends-bitter-green-iron-battle-with-element-zero/) | startup | Australian startup (ex-Fortescue scientists) using electrochemical reduction with ionic-liquid electrolytes to make green iron and other metals from ores. | Survived a two-year Fortescue trade-secrets lawsuit settled November 2025 with damages claims dismissed and cost orders vacated; described in court reporting as financially strained |

> **White space:** Electrowinning's low current densities mean enormous cell area and capex per tonne — the physics that kept it out of ironmaking for a century — so only three credible players exist. The opening is pairing cells with cheap curtailed renewables (the process tolerates intermittency, unlike furnaces) and selling ultra-pure iron into premium niches like powders and electrical steel before attacking bulk steel.

**Flash and single-step novel ironmaking reactors** · `🔵 pilot`

Fine iron ore concentrate reacts in seconds with hydrogen, methane, or concentrated laser/electric heat in a single vessel, skipping the pelletizing/sintering and multi-unit flowsheets that add cost and emissions to conventional and H2-DRI routes.

|---|---|---|---|

| [Limelight Steel](https://www.canarymedia.com/articles/green-steel/us-awards-28m-for-cutting-edge-tech-to-clean-up-iron-and-steel) | startup | Oakland, CA startup building a laser furnace that reduces iron ore to molten iron with roughly half the energy and >80% lower emissions than a blast furnace. | $2.9M ARPA-E award (part of a 2025 $28M federal iron/steel tech package); pre-seed/seed stage |
| [Calix](https://www.esgtoday.com/rio-tinto-invests-23-million-in-green-iron-plant-pauses-bioiron-project/) | scaleup | ASX-listed company whose ZESTY electric flash calciner makes hydrogen-DRI directly from iron ore fines without agglomeration. | Rio Tinto signed a joint development agreement and invested $23M (Nov 2025) in the proposed ZESTY green iron demonstration plant at Kwinana, WA, alongside ARENA support |

> **White space:** Only ~4% of seaborne ore is DR-pellet grade, so fines-tolerant single-step reactors attack a real $50-80/t pellet-premium gap — yet the field is tiny because high-temperature fines handling and product consistency are unforgiving, and the University of Utah flash-ironmaking lineage (DOE-funded since the 2010s) still has no commercial operator. Room remains for new reactor entrants with capital-light pilots, as Hertha's $17M-to-1tpd economics demonstrated.

**Advanced alloy scrap sorting and metals recovery** · `🟡 scaling`

Sensor fusion (LIBS, XRF, X-ray transmission) plus AI classifies and separates mixed metal scrap into specific alloy grades at belt speed, letting 'twitch' and zorba streams that were exported be re-melted domestically into high-value wrought alloys with ~95% energy savings versus primary.
