## Buildings

Heating, cooling, and constructing buildings without fossil fuels: heat pumps in every form factor, envelope and windows, smart controls, low-carbon materials, and the refrigerant transition. Most of this technology is mature — the constraint is delivery, financing, and demand, which is exactly where the newest companies cluster.

### Air-Source Heat Pumps & Electrified HVAC

### Refrigerants & Commercial Refrigeration

**Low-GWP refrigerant chemistry and production (HFOs, low-GWP blends)** · `🟢 mature`

Fluorochemical producers synthesize hydrofluoroolefins (HFOs) and low-GWP blends that drop into air conditioning and refrigeration equipment with 90-99% lower warming potential than the HFCs being phased down under the AIM Act and EU F-gas rules, making this the fastest lever for cutting refrigerant emissions at scale.
