**Solar-integrated EVs** · `🔵 pilot`

Ultra-efficient vehicles with photovoltaic body panels that harvest enough sunlight to add daily driving range and cut charging dependence — viable only on very light, aerodynamic designs because car surfaces cap on-board PV at roughly 1 kW.

| Company | Type | What they do | Signal / status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Aptera Motors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aptera_(solar_electric_vehicle)) | startup | Three-wheeled ~10 mi/kWh composite solar EV claiming up to ~40 miles/day from sunlight alone. | $130M+ crowdfunded plus $75M equity line (Oct 2025); validation assembly line running 2025; first sales pushed to late 2026, volume production ~2028 pending ~$60M more |
| [⚠️ Lightyear (Atlas Technologies)](https://cars.usnews.com/cars-trucks/features/lightyears-parent-company-declares-bankruptcy) | startup | Built the €250k Lightyear 0, the first production solar car, then pitched a €40k Lightyear 2 before running out of money. | Parent declared bankruptcy Jan 2023 weeks after first deliveries; ~40k Lightyear 2 pre-orders never fulfilled; remnants pivoted to licensing solar roof tech · **☠ shut down** |
| [Sono Motors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sono_Motors) | startup | Canceled its crowdfunded Sion solar car after insolvency and pivoted to retrofit solar kits for buses and trucks (customers include Mitsubishi Europe, CHEREAU, Scania, MAN). | Sion program scrapped Feb 2023 with ~45k reservations; relaunched post-insolvency as a solar-retrofit supplier; Sion prototypes auctioned Aug 2025 |
| [Squad Mobility](https://www.squadmobility.com/) | startup | Dutch maker of the Squad Solar City Car, a sub-€10k L6e/LSV quadricycle whose roof panel adds ~20 km/day of urban range. | First customer deliveries in the Netherlands from 2024-25; targeting US LSV market |
| [Hyundai (solar roof options)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Ioniq_5) | incumbent | Offers factory solar-roof options on models like the Ioniq 5 and Sonata — solar as a supplemental feature rather than primary energy source. | Solar roof adds only ~1,300-2,000 km/yr; remains a low-take-rate option |

> **White space:** Both flagship solar-car startups failed (Lightyear bankrupt, Sono's Sion canceled) and Aptera is still pre-revenue after years — physics caps on-vehicle PV near 1 kW, so a standalone 'solar carmaker' only pencils with extreme lightweighting. The credible opening is solar as a feature: OEM roof options, retrofit kits for fleets, and sun-rich-market microcars/LSVs where 20-40 km/day of free range covers most trips.

**ICE-to-EV passenger conversions & classic-car retrofits** · `🟠 demonstration`

Specialists that remove combustion drivetrains from existing or classic cars and install electric ones, extending vehicle life and avoiding the embodied emissions of a new car — economically viable today mainly at luxury restomod price points.

| [Lunaz Design](https://www.electrive.com/2024/03/19/lunaz-applied-technologies-goes-into-administration/) | startup | Beckham-backed luxury electrification of Rolls-Royce, Bentley, Aston Martin and Range Rover classics at Silverstone. | Sister company Lunaz Applied Technologies (electric bin lorries) entered administration Mar 2024, pausing the group; Lunaz Design resumed restructured operations May 2024 |
| [⚠️ Transition-One](https://journalauto.com/services/retrofit-clap-de-fin-pour-transition-one/) | startup | French pioneer of certified low-cost (~€5-8k) retrofit kits for mass-market cars like the Twingo and Fiat 500; helped write France's retrofit regulation. | Judicial liquidation Mar 2023 after a €5M raise failed — founder blamed lack of industrialization financing · **☠ shut down** |

| [EV West](https://www.topspeed.com/ice-cars-to-ev-conversion-companies/) | startup | Long-running California shop supplying conversion components and Tesla-crate-motor swaps that seeded the US DIY conversion ecosystem. | Operating since 2010s with multi-year build backlog; parts business serves hundreds of independent converters |

> **White space:** Mass-market retrofit has repeatedly failed (Transition-One liquidated despite French subsidies and a regulation written around it) because kit-plus-labor cost rivals a used BEV; only luxury restomods clear margins. The opening is standardized, type-approved kits for a handful of iconic high-volume models installed through OEM dealer networks (the Renault/R-FIT pattern), or subsidy-backed fleet retrofits where duty cycles are predictable.
