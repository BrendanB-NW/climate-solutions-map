**Enhanced geothermal systems (EGS)** · `🟠 demonstration`

Creates artificial reservoirs by drilling into hot dry rock and hydraulically fracturing it with shale-oilfield tools, letting geothermal work far beyond natural hot springs; Fervo's plants and DOE's Utah FORGE testbed have cut drilling times and costs sharply since 2023, making EGS the highest-momentum next-gen pathway.

**Closed-loop / advanced geothermal systems (AGS)** · `🟠 demonstration`

Circulates a working fluid through sealed deep wellbores that gather heat by conduction alone — no fracking, no water consumption, and siting almost anywhere — but the physics of conduction-limited heat transfer means economics hinge on drilling very long laterals very cheaply.

**Superhot rock geothermal** · `🔵 pilot`

Targets rock above ~374°C where water becomes supercritical and each well can deliver 5-10x the power of a conventional well, potentially making geothermal cost-competitive almost anywhere on Earth — but drilling, casing, and downhole electronics at those temperatures are still unproven.

### Hydro & Marine Energy

**Conventional & run-of-river hydropower (incl. modernization/uprating)** · `🟢 mature`
